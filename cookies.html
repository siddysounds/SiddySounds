Cookie Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Cookies and similar storage

Cookies are small values stored by your browser. Local storage is another browser storage mechanism. This page explains the uses verified in this site's code. Hosting providers and third-party destinations may have their own storage practices.

Your control

Optional Google Analytics is off until you explicitly accept it. “Reject optional” leaves the main site, music, links and games available. Use the persistent Privacy settings button to review or withdraw your choice. The choice lasts 180 days unless you clear storage or the consent version changes. A Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal disables optional analytics. Continuing to browse is not consent.

Withdrawal and existing cookies

Rejecting or withdrawing disables Analytics in this implementation and attempts to delete accessible first-party Analytics and old Clarity cookies. Withdrawal after Analytics has loaded refreshes the page to stop its running scripts. It does not undo data already sent. The site cannot delete third-party or inaccessible cookies. Use browser controls to remove those. Microsoft Clarity/session replay, advertising tags, login cookies and advertising-personalization features are not loaded by this site's current pages.

External content and browser permissions

YouTube and social links open external sites, where their own cookie choices apply. The separate devotional video page loads a YouTube privacy-enhanced embed only after you choose to load it; once loaded, YouTube may use cookies or similar technologies. The Blogger feed, Firebase countdown, libraries, fonts and game assets can make requests to their providers; a request can disclose your IP address and request metadata even without a cookie. Weather and microphone features run only when you request them, with permission where needed. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Browser controls and contact

You can clear cookies and site data or restrict storage in your browser settings. Clearing site data also clears saved game preferences and privacy choices. If storage is blocked, a choice may last only for the current page. For questions or complaints, email mail@siddysounds.com. Material changes will be reflected here and, where required, a new optional-tracking choice will be requested.
