Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who operates this site

SiddySounds is the personal music and creative website operated by Sidharath Suri (Sid Suri). For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email mail@siddysounds.com. This notice covers www.siddysounds.com, including the games and weather page.

Information and purposes

  • Website delivery and security: GitHub Pages hosts the site. Hosting and network providers receive technical request information such as IP address, user agent, requested URL and timestamps to deliver and protect the website. Their own policies govern their service logs.
  • Messages you choose to send: The contact form opens your own email app. Nothing is submitted through a site database. If you send the email, I receive its content, address and email metadata, and use it to respond and manage the enquiry. Email providers process that message too. The site does not automatically subscribe you to a mailing list.
  • Optional analytics: Only after an explicit opt-in, the main pages may load Google Analytics 4 to measure page views, referral sources, approximate location and device/browser information. Online identifiers and usage data can be personal data. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled in this site's configuration. Microsoft Clarity, session replay and advertising tags are not loaded.
  • Requested content: The blog loads a public Blogger feed and the homepage release countdown may read a public date from Google Firebase. These providers receive technical request information. Third-party libraries, fonts and some game assets may be delivered by their providers. These connections are separate from optional analytics.
  • Weather: Weather is fetched only when you request it. Loading Delhi weather sends fixed city coordinates to Open-Meteo. Choosing “Use my location” asks your browser for permission and sends the returned coordinates to Open-Meteo to provide the forecast. The site does not save those coordinates. You can use fixed-city weather without sharing your location.
  • Games: Some games remember scores or difficulty in your browser. The speech game works with typed guesses; microphone input starts only after you choose it and grant browser permission. Your browser's speech provider may process audio remotely; check its privacy settings. The site does not record or retain microphone audio.

Your choices and legal bases

Optional analytics relies on consent, which you can refuse or withdraw using the persistent Privacy settings button. A Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal disables optional analytics in this site's implementation. Where applicable, responding to enquiries and operating a secure, usable website relies on legitimate interests or steps you request before an agreement; legal obligations may require specific records. No optional tracking consent is inferred from browsing, sending a message or accepting a policy.

Recipients and international processing

Relevant providers include GitHub, Google (Analytics, Blogger and Firebase), content/CDN providers used by individual games, Open-Meteo when requested, and the email services used for correspondence. Providers may process information outside your country under their own arrangements. Links to Instagram, Facebook, X, Snapchat and YouTube take you to independent services governed by their own policies. Following a link is your choice; videos are not automatically embedded on the main pages. The separate devotional video page loads a YouTube privacy-enhanced embed only after you choose to load it, and YouTube then receives the video request.

Provider notices: GitHub, Google, Open-Meteo. These notices explain the providers' processing; they do not replace this site's responsibilities.

Retention and security

The privacy preference saved on your device expires after 180 days. Game settings remain until you or your browser clear them. Analytics cookies have provider-controlled lifetimes described in the Cookie Policy; Google Analytics reporting retention depends on the property's settings. Correspondence is kept while needed to resolve an enquiry, meet legal obligations or address a dispute, then reviewed for deletion. Service-provider logs follow the relevant provider's retention rules. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no website or email system can promise absolute security; please do not email passwords, payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Rights, requests and complaints

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to the appropriate supervisory authority. Email the contact above with the subject “Privacy request” or “Privacy complaint”. I may ask for proportionate identity verification and will respond within the period required by applicable law. Rights and exceptions vary by jurisdiction. Browser storage can also be removed directly through browser settings. This site does not operate a data-broker or personal-data sales service.

Children and changes

The site does not ask children to create accounts or provide identifying information. If you believe a child has sent personal information, contact me so it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, deleted. This notice may change as features or practices change; the update date will be revised. A material change to optional tracking will require a fresh choice where applicable. Continued browsing is not consent to optional tracking.
